01 / THE METHOD

Plan from the state
already on screen.

When a user changes the instruction, the next action may require releasing an object, adjusting a pose, or introducing a new entity. SEGUE makes these intermediate steps explicit.

01

Observe

PREVIOUS INSTRUCTIONKneel beside the rock and reach toward the bell.

NEW USER INSTRUCTIONPick up the brass bell and feel its cool surface.

02

Plan

Combine the current frame with the previous and updated instructions. Identify the preparation the target action needs.

SEGUE

TRANSITION DEPENDENCY SCHEMA

Establish the pose, position, or contact needed for the target action.

The planner selects only the roles needed for the observed state.

03

Continue

Generate under a short sequence of segue prompts, then return to the original target instruction.

BELL EXAMPLE · CONDENSED PLAN

  1. 1
    Reach & grip

    Establish contact with the existing bell.

  2. 2
    Lift slightly

    Raise the bell off the rock.

  3. 3
    Lift & feel

    Continue into the requested interaction.
